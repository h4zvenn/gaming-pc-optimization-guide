Trust Factor is Counter-Strike 2's matchmaking system that considers a player's history and behavior across CS2 and Steam.

Valve does not disclose the complete Trust Factor algorithm or provide a way to view your numerical Trust Factor score.

## 1. Communication and Player Behavior

- Avoid toxicity, insults, and unnecessary arguments.
- Use voice chat for useful team communication.
- Mute individual toxic players when necessary.
- Avoid provoking teammates or opponents.
- Never intentionally disrupt your team's gameplay.

Disabling all communication is not required. Constructive communication can improve teamwork.

## 2. Deathmatch and Casual Modes

Deathmatch and Casual are useful for practicing aim, movement, and game mechanics.

However, Valve has not confirmed that playing a specific number of matches in these modes improves Trust Factor or resets previous reports.

**There is no verified rule requiring 15–20 Deathmatch or Casual matches to restore Trust Factor.**

## 3. Commendations

Players can receive commendations for positive interactions.

However, Valve has not confirmed that exchanging commendations directly increases Trust Factor or offsets player reports.

Avoid treating commendation exchanges as a method of manipulating account reputation.

## 4. Steam Account Activity

Trust Factor considers information associated with a player's Steam account and Counter-Strike history.

Recommended practices:

- Use your own Steam account.
- Play legitimately.
- Follow Steam and CS2 rules.
- Keep your account secure.
- Avoid third-party software that interferes with the CS2 process.

There is no confirmed evidence that writing Steam reviews, rating Workshop items, or deleting negative profile comments directly improves Trust Factor.

## 5. Playing With Friends

Playing with reliable teammates may improve communication and coordination.

However, joining a party does not guarantee protection against reports or competitive cooldowns.

Valve states that party matchmaking considers the player with the lowest Trust Factor.

## 6. After a Competitive Cooldown

- Wait until the restriction expires.
- Check your internet connection.
- Avoid abandoning active matches.
- Do not remain AFK.
- Avoid excessive vote-kicking.
- Continue following the game's rules.

Standard Competitive Cooldowns have multiple levels. According to Steam Support, one week of clean play reduces the cooldown level by one.

**This does not guarantee an improvement in Trust Factor.**

## Conclusion

There is no verified method to instantly restore Trust Factor.

The best approach is legitimate gameplay, following community rules, avoiding toxic behavior, and participating normally in matches.

Be skeptical of guides promising guaranteed Trust Factor improvements through specific match counts, commendations, or waiting periods.
